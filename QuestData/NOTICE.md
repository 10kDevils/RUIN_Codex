# RUIN Codex quest rewards

QuestData/QuestRewards.lua lists the weapons and armour WoW Forever quests
give, for the Best in Slot tab. It is extracted from **QuestieDB 1.1.3**
(November 19, 2026), the Forever build (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB):
each item's quest rewards, type and required level, and each quest's name,
level, side, class and zone. Reward lists from the WoW Forever full catalog
of November 19, 2026 are added where the quest exists in QuestieDB.
